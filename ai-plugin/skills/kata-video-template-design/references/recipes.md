# Template recipes

## Short tips video (9:16, 30–45 s)
| # | Layout | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | title, pop, top | "3 mẹo …" | Hook question + promise |
| 2–4 | caption, slide-up, top | "1. …" + short detail | One tip per scene |
| 5 | title, pop | Call to action | "Lưu lại / theo dõi để …" |
Gradient backgrounds per scene, same angle (160°), different hues; captions karaoke at bottom.

## Product intro (16:9, 30–60 s)
| # | Layout | Background |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | title | product photo (placeholder), zoom-in, overlay 0.5 |
| 2 | list (3 benefits) | dark gradient |
| 3 | caption (offer) | product photo, pan-right |
| 4 | title (CTA + website) | solid brand color |
Watermark with the brand name; captions optional.

## Quote / inspiration (1:1, 15–30 s)
quote layout, typewriter animation, serif font, blurry transition 0.8 s, minDurationSeconds 5.

## Story / explainer (16:9 or 9:16, 60–120 s)
hook → context → 3–5 steps (caption or list) → summary → CTA. Mix media scenes (user photos) with text scenes
every 2–3 scenes to keep attention.
